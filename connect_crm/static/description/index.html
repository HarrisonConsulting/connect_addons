Twilio calls, in your CRM pipeline.

Every Connect call links to a CRM lead — by phone match, by UTM source, or by auto-creation rules you set. The lead's stat button counts calls; the call's reference jumps to the lead. The pipeline learns who is dialling.

Why

A telephony stack that doesn't write back to CRM is a black hole — calls happen, salespeople know, the pipeline doesn't. Connect CRM closes that loop. The call record carries a lead reference and a utm.source; the lead carries a stored count of its calls, indexed phone fields for fast match, and a stat button to drill in. Auto-create rules turn unmatched calls into leads on terms you control.

How it works

  1. Phone numbers get normalized
    Two stored, indexed fields — phone_normalized, mobile_normalized — are computed on the lead so call lookups stay fast at any volume.
  2. Inbound call hits open lead first
    On on_call_status, the call searches active leads in non-won stages by normalized number. If multiple match, the one with prior calls wins.
  3. UTM source matches the dialled number
    Add a phone number to a utm.source (one source per number, enforced by constraint). Inbound calls to that number tag the call and the lead.
  4. Auto-create when nothing matched
    Per direction, per status, plus an unknown-caller toggle and a default salesperson, decide whether the call spawns a lead or opportunity.
  5. Summaries land on the lead chatter
    When register summary is on, the call's transcript summary posts to the linked lead's chatter and the lead view reloads.

What's in the box

Lead-call link

A lead Many2one on connect.call, an inverse connect_calls on crm.lead, and the call record's ref resolves to the lead.

UTM source by phone

A phone field on utm.source with a UNIQUE constraint, plus list and form view inheritance so the column is visible in CRM reporting.

Auto-create rules

Eight settings split incoming and outgoing, answered and missed, plus an unknown-caller switch and a default salesperson. Lead or opportunity is a single dropdown.

Stat button on lead

A phone-icon stat button on the lead form opens a list of that lead's calls, scoped by [('lead', '=', active_id)].

Create lead from call

A button on the call form opens a new crm.lead with phone, partner, and source pre-filled; an unlink button reverses the link without touching the lead.

Active-calls popup patch

The OWL active-calls popup gets a click handler — clicking the lead pill on a live call opens the lead's form view in the current tab.

Compatibility

Odoo 19.0 crm utm connect

Depends on crm, utm, and connect. The Connect webhook security group gains create/read/write on crm.lead and read on stage, team, and alias-domain models so inbound webhooks can spawn leads safely.

Field note — opinionated about silent calls

The default rules auto-create leads for missed and unknown-caller calls because silence is the worst CRM signal. If your team prefers a quieter pipeline, the four answered/missed toggles let you tighten things; the default salesperson catches anything without an answering user. Tune once, stop reading the call log.
